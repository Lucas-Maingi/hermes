Knowledge base

Upload business policies, product guides, price lists, and FAQs. Hermes extracts text, then chunks and indexes it using BM25 plus vector similarity when embeddings are configured.

Supported: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, HTML, XML, YAML, RTF, and EML. Up to 15 MB per file. Scanned/image-only PDFs require OCR before upload. Original files are not retained; extracted text is indexed.

WhatsApp and payments

Credentials are encrypted at rest using HERMES_ENCRYPTION_KEY. They are write-only here and never returned to the browser. Do not paste test credentials into a public/shared machine.

Checks Meta account access and Daraja OAuth only; it will not send messages or payment prompts.

Approved WhatsApp templates

Create and get templates approved in WhatsApp Manager first. Hermes cannot submit them to or verify approval with Meta; only add a template here after confirming its exact name, language, category, and body variable count in Meta.

Customer messaging permission

Record permission only when you have evidence of the customer's opt-in. The customer can withdraw it at any time; scheduled messages are suppressed after opt-out. A STOP/UNSUBSCRIBE message also withdraws consent.

Schedule a WhatsApp follow-up

A running worker is required for delivery. Permission is checked again at send time. Template delivery also depends on live Meta credentials and its approval state there.
